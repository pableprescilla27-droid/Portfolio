About me

From PN scholar to QA engineer

I'm a proud recipient of a PN (Passerelles Numériques) scholarship, which gave me the opportunity to study Information Technology at the University of San Carlos in Cebu City. In school I learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP.

My horizons grew even further during my internship at WebriQ. There I learned Tailwind CSS, worked with Gatsby code, took part in content migration, analyzed problems, and became a better communicator with my teammates.

Today I work as a QA Software Engineer at Technology Group M Lhuillier Financial Services, Inc., where I test the MCash mobile app, the MCash web app and the M Lhuillier website. I care about the details, because every bug I catch is one a customer never has to face.
